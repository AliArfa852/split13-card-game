# Game Overview

Check! is a 2–6 player card game about *imperfect memory*. Each player holds four cards face-down in a 2×2 grid and only ever sees two of them, right at the start. From then on you're managing a hand you half-remember, trying to end the round with the **lowest total value**.

The tension: every turn is a chance to improve your hand, but also a chance to leak information to everyone else — and it happens on a short clock (see [[Turn Timers]]).

**Core loop:**
1. [[Turn Structure]] — draw, then discard.
2. [[Matching Stage]] — everyone gets a shot at dumping a same-rank card.
3. [[Special Abilities]] — K/Q/J discards trigger peek/swap effects.
4. Repeat until someone calls Check → [[Calling Check & Round End]] → [[Scoring]].
