# Draw Action

The current player must do exactly one of:

## 1. Draw from the Draw Pile
Take the top card, visible only to you. Then choose:
- **Swap & Discard** — put the drawn card face-down into one hand slot; the card that was there goes face-up onto the Discard Pile.
- **Discard Drawn Card** — put the drawn card straight onto the Discard Pile, face-up. Hand unchanged.

## 2. Draw from the Discard Pile
Only legal if the pile is **not sealed** (see [[Matching Stage]]) and its top card is **not locked**. You also **cannot** take a King/Queen/Jack this way. If legal: you take it, and **must** swap it into your hand (no "discard it again" option) — the card it replaces goes face-up onto the pile.

## Special-card note
If a K/Q/J is drawn from the *deck* and swapped into your hand, nothing happens — abilities only fire when a special card is **discarded**, never when it's merely acquired. This is what makes holding a King in your hand quietly dangerous: it's inert until it leaves your hand, at which point it's live for whoever ends up with it on top of the pile (you, via discard, or an opponent, via [[Matching Stage]]).
