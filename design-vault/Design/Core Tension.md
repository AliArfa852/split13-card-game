# Core Tension

Three tensions that seem to be doing most of the work, and are probably the things to protect (or deliberately break) in a redesign:

**1. Memory vs. information leakage.** Anything you do to learn about your own hand (peek) or improve it (swap, draw) is visible in outline to everyone else, even when its content isn't ([[Special Abilities]], [[Design Philosophy]]). You can't get information for free — only in a form other people can partially reconstruct.

**2. Speed vs. accuracy in [[Matching Stage]].** Matching is a race with a penalty for being wrong: fastest correct card wins, but any wrong guess costs you a card, and being wrong three times over the course of a round can flat-out disqualify you. That converts "I think I remember a 7 in my hand" into an actual bet, not just a mental note.

**3. Holding value vs. holding power.** J/Q/K are simultaneously the highest-value cards to be caught with ([[Card Values]]) and the only cards with abilities ([[Special Abilities]]). You want to play them for the effect, but every turn you don't is a turn you're sitting on 11–13 points of risk.

If a redesign mechanic doesn't touch at least one of these, it's probably closer to a skin change than a rules change — which isn't a criticism, just useful for scoping (see [[Redesign Ideas]]).
