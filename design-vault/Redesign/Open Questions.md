# Open Questions

## Split 13 (draft v1)

Places [[Split 13 - Game Rules]] filled a gap with a reasonable-sounding default. Confirm or override each:

1. **Team ↔ seat mapping.** Assumed seat 1 & 3 = Team A, seat 2 & 4 = Team B, with plain clockwise turn order (which is what produces the A1, B1, A2, B2 pattern you described). Is that right, or did you picture something else — e.g. teams re-forming each hand rather than being tied to a fixed seat?
2. **Table clears fully after every capture.** Assumed the next player always starts a brand-new stack from empty right after a capture — no leftover card, no carry-over. Confirm.
3. **Capturing ends your turn.** Assumed you don't get to throw a second card in the same turn just because you captured. Confirm.
4. **Tie-break.** What happens if Team A and Team B end a hand with equal points? Not specified yet — options include: sudden-death extra mini-hand, a shared/split win, or something else.
5. **Per-turn timer.** Check! times every decision window ([[Turn Timers]]) because there's a stalling risk around hidden-information decisions. Split 13's throw is a single obvious action (no hidden info to deliberate over) — do you still want a timer (e.g. so a human can't stall the table), or is this untimed for v1?
6. **Bot difficulty.** Currently one fixed, deliberately simple heuristic (§10 of the rules doc). Fine for v1, but flag if you want difficulty tiers (easy/normal/hard) before this gets built.
